# Sources consulted (2026-11-02)

1. OpenAI, *Quantitative lower bounds for trace reconstruction* (2026-09-24), OpenAI Math family #122. Formalization scope: https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/122.md . Manuscript repository: https://github.com/openai/math/tree/main/preprints/quantitative-lower-bounds-for-trace-reconstruction-September-24-2026 . Upstream scope targets asymptotic reconstruction and minimal one-trace TV, not the particular finite certificate here.
2. Chrisnata, Kiah, Rao, Vardy, Yaakobi, Yao, *On the Number of Distinct k-Decks: Enumeration and Bounds*, ISCIT 2019, DOI: 10.1109/ISCIT.2019.8905191. k-deck enumeration and subsequence counts are prior art.
3. Golm, Nahvi, Gabrys, Milenkovic, *The Gapped k-Deck Problem*, arXiv:2201.12671 (2022), https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12671 . Thue-Morse-related recursive constructions are prior art.

Limitations: This list is not a comprehensive novelty search; the literature is not exhaustively surveyed. In particular, equality of finite optimal polynomial envelopes elsewhere remains undetermined.
